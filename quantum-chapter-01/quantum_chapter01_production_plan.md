**How the times were measured:**
- `tools/timing.mjs --voice` found the speech blocks with ffmpeg `silencedetect`.
- A dynamic-programming alignment matched them, in order, to the 138 spoken sentences and headings of the script. It uses three cues:
  - sentence length;
  - pause depth;
  - the recording's ~0.8–1.0 s pauses before section headings.
- Three sentence starts were confirmed by hand from the pause map and pinned:
  - 0:08.1 "কল্পনা করুন…";
  - 0:40.1 "স্বাগতম…";
  - 6:21.3 "দুটি কিউবিটের ক্ষেত্রে…", where the four spoken kets |00⟩…|11⟩ are four separate speech blocks.
- The audio was not transcribed by a speech recogniser, so a boundary can be off by one short phrase where the narration runs two sentences together without a pause.

## 3. Visual style guide

**Palette**

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Background | Deep navy to black: #030712 → #071028, radial highlight #10245a |
| Primary accent (qubits, \|0⟩, constructive) | Cyan #22d3ee |
| Second accent (\|1⟩, entanglement links) | Violet #a78bfa |
| State vectors, H gate, destructive interference | Magenta #f472b6 |
| Correct / success | Green #34d399 |
| Misconception and caveat boxes | Amber #fbbf24 |
| Text | #e8f0ff, secondary text #9db0d6 |

**Typography**
- Bengali: Noto Sans Bengali (600/700 for headings).
- Latin and scientific terms: Inter.
- Code: JetBrains Mono.
- Equations: KaTeX (Computer Modern style).
- Minimum size: 22 px for on-screen notes and 38 px for subtitles at 1080p.

**Language rules**
- Bengali carries the explanations.
- English is used for standard terms, in a smaller secondary weight: qubit, Bloch sphere, Born rule, CNOT, Qiskit.
- Mathematical symbols and code stay in their standard form.

**Layout**
- Chrome: the series name sits top-left and the current section top-right.
- Each section opens with a full-screen numbered card while the heading is spoken.
- Content sits between y = 130 and y = 900; the subtitle band sits at the bottom.

**Motion**
- Elements fade in and rise 20 px over 0.7 s, starting on the sentence that introduces them.
- Scenes cross-fade over 0.6–0.7 s.
- Continuous motion is slow: Bloch camera ±7°, cloud ~10°/s, particles drifting upward.
- No fast cuts, no shake.

**Scientific imagery**
- Superposition is shown as amplitudes and state vectors, never as an object physically in two places.
- Measurement always shows two possible outcomes and statistics over many shots.
- Illustrative data is labelled as such.

## 4. Editing and audio-synchronisation instructions

1. **Main track:** use `output/voiceover.mp3` unchanged, with no time-stretch, trims, gain riding, EQ or noise reduction. The film starts at 0:00.0 with the first word and ends at 11:11.8 with the last.
2. **Cue sheet:** place visuals from the narration table above, or import `output/quantum_chapter01_subtitles_bn.srt`, which carries the same times.
   - Reveal each element on the sentence that introduces it.
   - Section cards cover the spoken heading.
3. **Music bed:** keep it 24 dB below the narration's speech level, ducking a further 6 dB under speech (`tools/make_audio.py`). No music stingers over words.
4. **Sound effects:** use soft whooshes at scene changes only, at least 20 dB under the voice, and a quiet chime at the close.
5. **Mix target:** voice peaks are untouched (the mix only lowers everything if the sum would clip). Check intelligibility on laptop speakers and headphones.
6. **Re-rendering:** after any change to the visuals, run:
   ```bash
   node tools/timing.mjs --voice output/voiceover.mp3
   node tools/make_srt.mjs && node tools/check.mjs
   python3 tools/make_audio.py --voice output/voiceover_48k.wav
   node tools/render.mjs
   ```
